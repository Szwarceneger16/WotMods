Stay Sharp bundled voice

English: "Med kit is ready."
Synthesized offline with CMU Flite 2.2, cmu_us_slt voice.
Polish, Russian, French, German, Spanish and Ukrainian: synthesized offline
with eSpeak NG 1.53.0 (source revision ba90c8e), standard language voices,
180 words/minute. Resampled to 16 kHz mono PCM using FFmpeg; no speech model
or executable is included. Text and generation settings: assets/voices/manifest.json
in the source repository. Output speech is synthetic.
eSpeak NG project: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
The audio is synthetic; no game or actor recording is redistributed.
No synthesizer or voice-model library is shipped or required by the mod.
Project: https://github.com/festvox/flite
