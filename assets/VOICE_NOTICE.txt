Generation and processing:
- Local CPU inference inside a network-disabled rootless container.
- Determination and projected-voice control tags; no voice-cloning prompt.
- Pitch-preserving tempo +7% using FFmpeg atempo=1.07, constant gain to
  approximately -20 LUFS, then mono 16-bit PCM at 16 kHz, under two seconds.
- No intercom filter, added ambience, reverb or pitch shift.
- Ukrainian was generated in an explicit Ukrainian sentence context;
  only the complete ready announcement was extracted for the package.
- Source/model/codec revisions, seeds, generation settings, crop details,
  full WAV and PCM checksums are in assets/voices/manifest.json.

Local speech recognition checked the wording. Polish and English were
auditioned by the project owner. This does not certify every language's
accent, naturalness, native bank loading or volume inside the game.
