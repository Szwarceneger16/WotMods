Stay Sharp ready voices - AI-generated neural speech

English, Russian, French, German, Spanish and Ukrainian were synthesized
with Piper 1.8.0 / neural VITS. Polish was synthesized with Resemble AI's
Chatterbox Multilingual V2 (chatterbox-tts 0.1.7).
Only finished PCM audio is distributed. No speech engine, ONNX runtime,
model weights, network service or telemetry is included in the game mod.

Processing: normal speaking speed, outer silence trim, peak normalization
to 0.82 and resampling to 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM. No time compression.
Source/model/config/WAV hashes and generation settings:
assets/voices/manifest.json in the source repository.

https://storage.googleapis.com/chatterbox-demo-samples/mtl_prompts/pl_m.flac

watermarker was used; no watermark-removal step was applied. Watermark
detection after resampling has not been tested.
