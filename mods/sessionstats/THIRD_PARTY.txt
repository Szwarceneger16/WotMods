4. Game resources and names
World of Tanks is a Wargaming game. The mod uses fonts supplied by the running
client. Thumbnails reference vehicle/map resources installed in the client.
Copied fonts, garage artwork and client files are not bundled. PODGLAD.html
examples are synthetic and are not the user's statistics.
