# Concise group help and centered statistics in 0.9.22

Group-information help uses a short explanation instead of a long collection
of table details. It keeps the distinction between a group's Combined WN8 and
the average of its individual battle ratings clear without filling the panel
with text.

Base-credit Min/Max source actions are disabled when the corresponding Base
coverage is incomplete for the selected period and group. Choosing a period
with complete coverage makes those actions available again. Availability is
based on that selection rather than an unrelated missing result elsewhere in
the saved history. Missing Base values retain their unavailable display.

Aggregate and upper-summary numeric values are centered in their own cells.
Source arrows remain beside their figures, and the shared settled Costs value
uses its existing combined credit cell. Table labels retain their single-line
caption treatment and full-caption help.

The WN8-colored card treatment gains a stronger layered glow for depth. Its
border stays thin, so the added emphasis comes from the glow rather than a
thicker card outline.

These notes do not claim live-client verification or a completed validation
result.
